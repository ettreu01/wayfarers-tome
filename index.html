Story & Character

Origins

Kiran Silverbough is a 106-year-old Wood Elf from Sylvaeren. His parents, Caelan and Liora Silverbough, disappeared on an expedition when he was 62. His grandfather Thalion raised him afterward.

At age 100, Kiran secretly left his community to escape the expectations of its elders. After six years on the road, his travels have brought him to Bellwether's centennial survival festival.

He is stealthy, sarcastic, quick with a quip, and happiest when he can keep his distance and let his bow do the talking.
